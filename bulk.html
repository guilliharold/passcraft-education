Bulk Passwords

For ICT staff and technicians: your file is processed locally within this browser and is not uploaded.

How does this work?
  1. Choose a .csv or .xlsx file with a column titled Password.
  2. PassCraft fills that column with a different password for each person.
  3. Preview the result, then download it as a new file.

Your file stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

Other columns are kept as they are. An .xlsx download loses fonts, colours and other formatting, and includes only the sheet you choose.

    💡 Good to know: the word-based styles are easy to remember, so they suit first-time passwords, but they are weaker than long random ones. Random characters are stronger but harder to type (see the Disclaimer). Wherever you can, require a password change at first sign-in and turn on multi-factor authentication. Only handle student details in ways your school’s policies allow.
