Password Safety Tips
🚫

Never Use These

These are the most commonly guessed passwords — avoid them completely!

password 123456 qwerty abc123 monkey letmein iloveyou admin
✅

Great Examples

Memorable, colourful, and secure — passwords and a passphrase to inspire you!

CleverTealRocket83@ BraveBlueDingo47! SunnyCoralFalcon29$ clever-mountain-falcon-teal47!
🔁

Don't Reuse Passwords

If one account gets hacked and you reuse the same password, ALL your accounts are at risk. Use a unique password for each site or app.

🤫

Keep It Secret

Never share your password — not even with friends! Your teacher or school IT team will never ask for your password.

📏

Longer = Stronger

Every extra word or character multiplies the guesses an attacker needs. PassCraft passwords are always at least 12 characters, and passphrases of 5+ words are even stronger.

🎨

Make It Memorable

The Adjective + Colour + Animal and Adjective + Colour + Noun patterns create a vivid mental image — like a picture in your head — that's easy to recall later!

📊

What the strength meter means

The meter estimates how many guesses an attacker would need, shown as “bits of randomness” (every extra bit doubles the guesses). Fair protects against someone guessing at a login page that locks after wrong tries. Strong and above also holds up better if a password database is stolen. For your email or other important accounts, use a passphrase of 5 or more words.

🌐 Using PassCraft: This tool runs entirely in your browser — no passwords are stored, sent, or saved anywhere. It is completely private!
