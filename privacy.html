Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Passwords

Passwords you generate with the Generator or test with the Strength Indicator are handled only in your browser. PassCraft doesn’t send, save or log them, and we can’t see them. The History list clears when you close or reload the page, and the Strength Indicator field is wiped when you leave. The only exception is the optional Breach Check, explained below.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply to you. Please never include a password or details like your school, address or phone number. We keep messages only as long as needed to reply, then delete them.

Breach Check (optional)

The Breach Check page does nothing unless you tick the consent box and click Check for breaches, and you’re asked again for every check. When you do, your browser turns the password into a SHA-1 hash and sends only the first 5 characters of that hash to the Pwned Passwords service run by Have I Been Pwned. It replies with a list of hash endings that start the same way, and your browser searches that list on your own device. Your password and the full hash are never sent, and PassCraft doesn’t receive, see or store them or the result.

Have I Been Pwned and its hosting provider can see technical details, such as your IP address, when you check. They’re run by other organisations, may handle information outside Australia, and have their own privacy policy.

Bulk Passwords

The Bulk Passwords page reads a file you choose only in your browser. The file is never uploaded, and PassCraft doesn’t save, log or see it or the passwords made for it. The file you download is saved on your own device and holds the passwords in plain text, so look after it and delete it when it’s no longer needed. If your file contains student details, handle them in the ways your school allows.

Cookies and tracking

No accounts, ads, analytics or cookies. Nothing is stored on your device.

Other services

The site’s fonts are hosted on the site itself, so loading them doesn’t involve any other company. Our web host can see technical details, such as your IP address, when a page loads. It is run by another organisation, may handle information outside Australia, and has its own policy.

Students under 18

You don’t need to give any personal information to use the Generator, Strength Indicator or Learn pages. If you’re under 18, please check with a parent, guardian or teacher before using the Breach Check.

Questions and complaints

PassCraft is an independent project by Harold Guilline. To ask what we hold, have it corrected or deleted, or make a privacy complaint, email support@passcraft-edu.com.
