Check a Password

Optional. Nothing is sent until you tick the box and click Check.

How does this work?
  1. Your browser scrambles the password into a hash.
  2. It sends only the first 5 characters of that hash to Have I Been Pwned, a free breach-checking service.
  3. The service replies with a list of possible matches.
  4. Your browser checks that list on your own device.

Your password is never sent, and PassCraft never sees it or the result.

Unlike the Strength Indicator, this tool is meant for a real password, because checking an example tells you nothing about the one you actually use. More in the Privacy Policy.
